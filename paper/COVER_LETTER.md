# Cover letter draft

Dear Editor,

We submit the research article, “Repair the Boundary, Not the Skills:
Contract-Guided Adapter Synthesis for LLM-Agent Skill Composition.” The study
addresses a software-maintenance problem in reusable LLM-agent systems: a chain
can fail at an interface even when its component Skills have individually
successful provenance. We evaluate whether one contract-guided boundary adapter
can restore native task success without editing the component artifacts.

The manuscript separates exposed development evidence from an untouched,
identifier-disjoint confirmation cohort; compares the method with no repair, a
contract-only local heuristic, whole-chain rollback, and three revision-pinned
open instruction models; and reports native ALFWorld and ScienceWorld execution,
task-clustered inference, provenance, locality, and action cost. Failed method
revisions and invalid source trajectories are retained in the artifact.

The work is original, is not under review elsewhere, and has been approved by
all authors. Replace this sentence if the authors cannot attest to it. Author,
funding, conflict-of-interest, data-license, and code-release details must be
completed before submission.

Sincerely,

Corresponding author (pending)
